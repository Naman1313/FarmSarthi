# 🌾 Farm Saarthi

*Farm Saarthi* is an innovative agricultural shopping platform developed during the *CodeVerse Hackathon* by *Team Syntax Syndicate. It aims to eliminate the inefficiencies of traditional *mandis (markets) by providing a direct digital interface between farmers and buyers. The project bridges the gap between the *Minimum Support Price (MSP)* and prices quoted by middlemen, empowering farmers with better price realization.



## 🚀 Site Deployment

🔗 [Live Site](https://farmsarthi.onrender.com)  



## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional mandis often lead to:

- Price exploitation of farmers  
- Lack of price transparency  
- Dependency on intermediaries  
- Uncertainty in weather may lead to crop destruction (rain, storms)  
- Regional inaccessibility  

*Farm Saarthi* replaces physical mandis with a digital marketplace that ensures:

- ✅ Fair pricing via MSP tracking  
- ✅ Direct buyer-seller interaction  
- ✅ Real-time price updates  
- ✅ Market access for even remote farmers  



## 🧠 Unique Features

- 🌐 *Multilingual Support* – Bridges language barriers for Indian farmers (Coming Soon) 
- ⚡ *Instant Buying & Selling* – For crops and seeds, improving speed and transparency  
- 📈 *Real-time Price Tracking* – Based on crop trends and MSP  
- 📊 *Market Trends* – Visual and easy-to-understand insights into crop pricing and demand
